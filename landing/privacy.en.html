Privacy Policy

Codex Remote Privacy Policy

This page explains privacy handling for Codex Remote (the "app"). This policy applies to app version 0.2.0.

Effective date: 2026-03-11

1) Personal information collected

Category Purpose
Session ID, server address, port, connection settings Relay/local connection management and quick reconnection
Prompts entered by user Passed to Codex service to generate responses
Error messages and usage logs (anonymized) Stability improvements and bug investigation
App settings (theme/options/history display) Personalized UI and user experience improvements
Device identifier, access IP May be processed temporarily during network transfer to support app execution, security, and misuse prevention.

2) Purposes of collection and use

  • Provide remote/local session connectivity
  • Send prompts and display streaming responses
  • Restore session history and manage sessions
  • Maintain app stability and handle bugs
  • Security and abuse prevention

3) Retention period

  • Local data: retained until app uninstall or manual deletion by the user
  • Session/log data: retained until operational purpose is met, or as required by law
  • Network logs: deleted immediately after the minimum necessary period

4) Third-party sharing and processing

  • Provided only when required by law or with user consent
  • This app does not include third-party analytics or ad SDKs as core services.
  • However, operating policies of browser/device runtime environments and relay servers may apply.

5) User rights

  • You may request access, correction, and deletion of retained data.
  • You may withdraw consent or request restriction of processing.
  • Local data deletion is handled through app settings, cache clear, or app uninstall.

6) Security controls

  • Relay/API traffic is handled over HTTPS.
  • Only necessary data is stored, avoiding plain-text storage of sensitive values.
  • Connection tokens and credentials should be controlled by the user.

7) Cookies and tracking

This app does not use separate cookie-based ad-tracking scripts by default. Standard access logs may still be generated depending on hosting and browser cache settings.

8) Children's privacy

This app is not intended for users under 14. If use by a minor under 14 is detected, consent from a guardian may be required.

9) Changes

This policy may be revised due to legal changes, service changes, or security/operations policy changes. The effective date will be updated when revised.
